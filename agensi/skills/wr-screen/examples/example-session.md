# Example session

**User:** Someone offered me Garrett Wilson for Drake London. Also, is Rashee Rice a buy?

**Skill runs:**

```
node scripts/wr-screen.js "Garrett Wilson" "Drake London" "Rashee Rice"
```

```
WR screen, 2026 season (top-24 PPR odds)

A       58.2%   30.4%  veteran  Garrett Wilson           last season 7g 59 tgt (NYJ)
A       58.2%   30.6%  veteran  Drake London             last season 12g 112 tgt (ATL)
A       58.2%   30.2%  veteran  Rashee Rice              last season 8g 78 tgt (KC)
```

**Skill output:**

| WR | Type | Grade | Top-24 odds | Share | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Garrett Wilson | veteran | A | 58.2% | 30.4% | 7 games, elite per-game role |
| Drake London | veteran | A | 58.2% | 30.6% | 12 games, elite per-game role |
| Rashee Rice | veteran | A | 58.2% | 30.2% | 8 games, elite per-game role |

- **Wilson for London:** same band, same odds. The trade is even on role. Decide on
  other factors (your RB/WR depth, schedule), not on which WR is "better".
- **Rice:** band A on only 8 games. His season-total share would read about 14% (band D),
  which is why some managers will sell him cheap. The per-game role is elite — a buy if
  the price reflects the missed games.
