What to actually do

Four changes, all free.

2026 priorities, in order of expected points

  1. Start what you pick up. The single highest-leverage change available. You are already finding the right players — a new add going straight to the bench for six weeks is the exact failure mode that cost you the most.
  2. Front-load your start/sit attention. Weeks 1–4 hold over half your season-long leak. Early-season is when projections are least trustworthy and your process is weakest at the same time.
  3. Target Nico Suave in trades. Fourth-best team, 6–8 record, unluckiest in the league. Their standings page is doing your negotiating for you.
  4. Discount Lamar-a-lago's 10–4. Sixth-best team by real strength. Don't overpay to buy from a team the schedule flattered.

Closing half the bench gap is worth about 111 points — roughly half a win of margin, at a cost of zero draft capital, zero FAAB and zero trades. It is the cheapest upgrade available to you, and it is entirely a habit.
